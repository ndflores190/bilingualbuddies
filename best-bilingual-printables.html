How to pick the right printables

Both languages on every page. The best packs put English and Spanish side by side, so kids see both at once instead of flipping between separate pages.

Match the age. Ages 4-7 need big letters, simple words, coloring, tracing, and counting. Older kids can handle reading passages and crosswords.

Print at home. Look for US Letter (8.5 x 11 in) PDFs you can print as many times as you like — one purchase should cover every child in the house or classroom.

Start free, then buy what you love. Try a free pack first — like our free Dando Gracias mini-pack — and buy the full packs for the topics your kids ask for again.
